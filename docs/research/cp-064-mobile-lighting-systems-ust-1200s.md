## 3. Working principle

A mobile light tower raises luminaires above a work area and supplies electrical energy from a diesel generator, battery, photovoltaic system, hybrid arrangement or a confirmed combination. Actual night operation depends on lighting load, energy storage, solar input, weather, controls and site conditions.

- insufficient solar charging conditions
