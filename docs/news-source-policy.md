# News source and originality policy

## Purpose

This is a local research and draft-production workflow for COWIN MACHINE. It collects relevant candidates; it does not claim to cover all global news and it does not publish automatically.

## Source hierarchy

1. Government, standards bodies, associations and research institutions.
2. Original project, manufacturer, mining-company or listed-company announcements.
3. Authoritative industry media.
4. Academic journals and conferences.
5. Credible mainstream media.

## Non-negotiable controls

- Confirm author/source, publication date, original URL and relevance before marking a candidate eligible.
- Use at least two independent external sources published within the active 90-day window for a draft.
- Do not use a competitor marketing page as the sole source of any fact.
- Never copy external titles, paragraphs, charts, photographs, logos, PDFs, certificates or customer stories.
- Do not present another company, project or customer as COWIN MACHINE.
- Treat all third-party images as not usable unless an explicit commercial license is separately recorded.
- Keep source citations factual and short. Write all customer-facing analysis from scratch.

## Draft gate

A topic may enter the local draft generator only when it has two independent eligible recent sources, one verified COWIN MACHINE product-family link, three or more valid internal links, a unique similarity key and no unresolved factual conflict. Publication requires an explicit separate approval.

## Required review

Review title/meta/H1 differentiation, source date window, fact-to-source mapping, links, FAQ truthfulness, schema fields, image rights and plagiarism/similarity before any publication request.
