A practical connection between buyers and magnetic separation supply.

BZMAGNET is a trading and export coordination business for magnetic separation equipment. We coordinate product information, commercial communication and export order requirements with buyers and supply partners.

Supply and export coordination

Our scope may include enquiry clarification, equipment category matching, quotation coordination, order documentation, packing requirements and shipment communication. Equipment suitability and final technical details must be confirmed against the actual application.

What to send for a quote

  • Material name, composition and process position
  • Required capacity and particle size range
  • Moisture, temperature and other operating conditions
  • Target metal or separation objective
  • Installation dimensions, photos or drawings
  • Power standard, destination and required delivery terms
